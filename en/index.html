Service

What Hi Coffer does

It finds digital assets (identification), decides what (classification) goes into the vault (distinction) and how it is stored (management/maintenance) (vaulting).The digital vault protects the user's digital assets directly and indirectly, and enables their safe use.

1

Identification

The number/size/attributes of folders and files on storage media and devices connected to the device, and the device status, are checked.

2

Classification

Files are divided into standard categories by nature and type.

3

Distinction

Duplicates, old versions, authentic-copy candidates and locations of sensitive data are identified.

4

Management/Maintenance

Items with harmful attributes (phishing/decoy/corrupted) and lost/damaged items are quarantined, then verified/acted on by the user.

5

Vaulting

Only digital assets (data/information) chosen/selected by the user are stored with encryption/original fingerprinting (SHA-256).
※ The vault key is owned/kept solely by the user.

Management basis · Hi DAM

A standard classification system for digital asset management.Asset classification, attributes and history are managed on all devicesunder the same (identical) system.

Digital vault

Only assets chosen by the user are stored.Original verification (fingerprint) and access records are kept.

Target users

It starts with individuals and families.It will expand to businesses and public institutions.

How it works

Management system

Steps 1–6 are diagnosis,steps 7–12 are execution, management and vault.Every process follows the user's confirmation, decision and approval, and presupposes preservation and recovery of originals.

  1. Install · Consent
  2. Device registration
  3. Identification scan
    Read-only
  4. Distinction analysis
    Content fingerprint
  5. Classification
  6. Diagnostic report
  7. User decision
  8. Organization run
    Reconfirm → Quarantine
  9. Confirm · Destroy
  10. Management · Maintenance
    Periodic check
  11. Vaulting
    Optional
  12. Use
    View · Submission copy

Processed on your PC

File content is processed on the user's PC.File content is not sent to a server.

No immediate deletion

The order is quarantine/move → user confirmation → deletion.Only the user can delete permanently.

Undo

Every change is recorded before and after execution.The initial structure can be restored at any time.

Protected areas untouched

Installation files, digital certificates and system areasare excluded from organization.

User-defined choices first

Device roles defined by the usertake precedence over organization rules.

AI only suggests

AI judgments are shown as suggestions with their grounds.The user decides whether to execute.
